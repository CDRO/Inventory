Gamification

XP, levels, quests and streaks are entirely optional. Turning this off never changes how any inventory feature works, for you or anyone else in your storages.

Holiday mode

Mark a week you'll be away so it pauses your streak instead of breaking it. Up to 8 weeks in any rolling year. Only this week or a future week can be marked; a marked week can only be un-marked while it is still in the future.

This storage

Expiry notifications

Off unless you turn it on. One short daily digest — what has expired and what is about to — posted to whatever URL you put below. Nothing is sent on a day with nothing to report, and no photos are ever sent.

This is meant for a notification service you run yourself, on your own network. Nothing here checks that: a public URL would put your product names, quantities and locations wherever it points.
